iRecipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

iRecipe ("the app") is a recipe and meal-planning app. This policy explains

what the app does with your information. It describes the app as it is built

and shipped; if anything below changes, this page changes with it.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to use iRecipe.
  • Your recipes, shopping lists, meal plans and settings are stored on your

device, not on our servers.

  • Two features send data off your device when you use them: the ingredient

scanner and Ask Chef. Both send to Google's Gemini API for processing.

  • We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party

advertising or tracking SDKs.

What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Recipes you save, import or create, and the photographs attached to them.
  • Shopping lists, including any prices and budget you enter.
  • Meal plans and collections.
  • Dietary preferences, allergies, household size and app settings.
  • Your subscription status, as reported by the App Store or Google Play.

You can export all of this at any time from Settings, and delete all of it from

Settings. Deleting the app also removes it.

What is sent off your device, and when

Ingredient scanning. When you photograph ingredients and ask the app to

analyse them, the photograph is sent to the Google Gemini API, which returns a

list of possible ingredients. Before sending, the app resizes the image and

removes EXIF metadata, including any location recorded by your camera. The

photograph is not stored by us. Alongside the image the app may send short

on-device hints it read from the picture, such as packaging text, barcode

numbers and broad image labels.

Ask Chef. The questions you type, the recent messages in that conversation,

and context assembled on your device are sent to the Google Gemini API to

produce a reply. That context can include your saved dietary preferences,

your recorded allergies and the names of items you have ticked off your

shopping list, so the answer suits you. Conversations are not stored on our

servers.

Recipe generation. When you ask the app to create recipes from confirmed

ingredients, those ingredient names and your preferences are sent to the same

API.

Recipe pictures. When a generated recipe has no photograph, the app may ask

an image model for an illustrative picture of that dish. Only the recipe's

title, cuisine and ingredient names are sent.

Google processes this data as our service provider under the Gemini API terms.

See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Recipe catalogue. Browsing categories fetches public recipe data from

TheMealDB (https://www.themealdb.com). These requests carry no personal data.

Importing a recipe link. If you paste a recipe URL, the app fetches that

page to read its recipe data. The request goes to the website you chose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple or Google. We never see your

payment details. The app receives only whether a subscription is active.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app records anonymous feature-usage events, for example that a category was

opened or that a chat reply arrived, along with counts. It never records the

content of your messages, your photographs, your recipes or your lists. In the

standard build these events stay on the device. If a build is configured with

our optional backend, the same events are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase

and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics.

Tracking and advertising

iRecipe shows no advertisements, contains no advertising SDK, and does not

track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

iRecipe is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal

information from children.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph ingredients for the scanner.
  • Photo library: only when you choose an existing photograph for a scan or

a recipe.

You can refuse or revoke either permission; the rest of the app continues to

work.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account.
  • Avoid sending anything off your device by not using the scanner, Ask Chef or

recipe generation.

  • Export or delete all of your data from Settings.
